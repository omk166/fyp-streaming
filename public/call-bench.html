Call benchmark: play into a meeting app, record what arrives

Plays the call benchmark's recording (noisy speech) into the microphone of the app under test, and records what the other participant of the call receives. All audio stays in this browser; the recording is saved to your computer for npm run call:bench:score. Set-up steps for Zoom, Meet and Teams are in the README, section "Call benchmark".

1 · Set up

The list of devices needs microphone permission once. Nothing is recorded until you press Start.

2 · Run

Before you start: both participants are in the call, the sender's microphone is the "Play into" device, the receiver's speaker is the "Record from" device, and the receiver's own microphone is muted. Keep this tab open until the end.

3 · Save and score

The recording appears here when the run ends.

Save recording

The file is a stereo WAV: left is what arrived, right is what was played (so the scorer knows exactly when each clip started). Score one or more runs with:
npm run call:bench:score -- data/call-bench/<time> zoom-auto=~/Downloads/call-zoom-auto-….wav
